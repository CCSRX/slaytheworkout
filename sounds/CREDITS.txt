Box breathing marimba cues
==========================

Source: University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS).
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/Mismarimba.html

Instrument: Marimba, yarn mallets, pianissimo (pp), performed by
Andrew Thierauf, recorded 20 March 2013 in the anechoic chamber at
the University of Iowa.

Original file:
  Marimba.yarn.pp.C3B3.aif
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/sound%20files/MIS/Percussion/Marimba/

These are soft yarn-mallet notes in a low, warm register. Derived files
were trimmed to a single note, gently low-passed around 2.5 kHz,
peak-normalized, faded, and encoded as mono MP3 at 64 kbps:

  marimba-a3.mp3    — A3, inhale cue (higher)
  marimba-fs3.mp3   — F#3, hold cue (middle)
  marimba-d3.mp3    — D3, exhale cue (lower)

downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions.”
