Box breathing pizzicato cues
============================

Source: University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS), 2012 violin recordings.
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISviolin2012.html

Instrument: Violin (Nicolai Tambovsky, 1998), performed by Leonid Iogansen,
recorded 11 December 2011 in the anechoic chamber at the University of Iowa
(Earthworks QTC40, 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono).

Original file:
  Violin.pizz.pp.sulG.C4A4.mono.aif

These are piano (pp) pizzicato (plucked) notes. Derived files were trimmed to a
single pluck, peak-normalized, faded, and encoded as mono MP3 at 64 kbps:

  violin-pizz-a4.mp3   — A4, inhale cue (higher)
  violin-pizz-fs4.mp3  — F#4, hold cue (middle)
  violin-pizz-d4.mp3   — D4, exhale cue (lower)

License: The University of Iowa states that these recordings “may be
downloaded and used for any projects, without restrictions.”
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
