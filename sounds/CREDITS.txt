Box breathing soundscape
========================

Source: University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (MIS), 2012 violin recordings.
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MIS.html
  https://theremin.music.uiowa.edu/MISviolin2012.html

Instrument: Violin (Nicolai Tambovsky, 1998), performed by Leonid Iogansen,
recorded 11 December 2011 in the anechoic chamber at the University of Iowa
(Earthworks QTC40, 16-bit 44.1 kHz mono).

Original files:
  Violin.arco.pp.sulG.G3B3.mono.aif   (G3, B3)
  Violin.arco.pp.sulD.D4B4.mono.aif   (D4, F#4)
  Violin.arco.pp.sulG.C4B4.mono.aif   (E4)
  Violin.arco.pp.sulA.A4B5.mono.aif   (A4)
  Violin.pizz.pp.sulG.C4A4.mono.aif   (F#4 pizzicato)

These are piano (pp) notes, arco unless noted. Derived files were trimmed to a
single note, high-pass filtered, level-matched, and encoded as mono MP3 at 64 kbps:

  violin-g3.mp3        — G3 arco (exhale / final chord root, low warmth)
  violin-b3.mp3        — B3 arco (Gmaj7 third)
  violin-d4.mp3        — D4 arco (drone and both chords)
  violin-e4.mp3        — E4 arco (Dadd9 ninth / inhale shimmer)
  violin-fs4.mp3       — F#4 arco (both chords)
  violin-a4.mp3        — A4 arco (drone and Dadd9)
  violin-pizz-fs4.mp3  — F#4 pizzicato (very soft tap at the start of each new cycle)
